# Scaevola Sales

**You know who you help. Scaevola Sales finds the ones who need you now.**

Your sales team in Claude. It finds buyers from public signals, drafts outreach about their situation in your voice, scores every lead and keeps your pipeline honest. It drafts; you decide and send. Nothing is sent, posted or promised without you.

Free and open, and every file lives in your own GitHub. **To start, you need a paid Claude plan and a free GitHub account.** The tools you already sell with plug in when you want them.

**Start here:** [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md) is the installer. Copy it into a new Claude chat and answer one question at a time. It copies the team's files from [sales/](sales/) into a private repository in your own GitHub and sets them up for your business.

**Built with Scaevola.** This is the sales team that reports to the CRO seat of the Scaevola executive team we run our own business on, with our details taken out. That executive team reviewed this release, seat by seat, before it went out. It's out in the open early on purpose, because GTM and AI move too fast to wait for perfect. Use it, push it, improve it, and share what you made in [Discussions](https://github.com/davidlionel/scaevola-sales/discussions). Every release makes it better for everyone.

## Run it on its own, or under your Scaevola team
- **On its own:** you're the decider, and the sales team reports to you.
- **Bolted on to [Scaevola](https://github.com/davidlionel/scaevola) (1.0.0 or later):** your CRO seat sets the direction, and the sales team runs the plays. It reads your house rules, customer profile, pricing rules and decisions from your Scaevola files, follows the stricter rule wherever the two differ, and reports to your CRO seat. It never changes your Scaevola files.

## The team
Eight assistants, each a plain file you can read and change:
- **Sales manager:** runs each business: which play next, the weekly review, what to keep, change or stop.
- **Strategist:** interviews you and writes the business down: who to sell to and their trigger, the offer, your voice, the targets.
- **Prospector:** finds the people who have the problem right now, from public signals, with a dated source on every row.
- **Fit scorer:** owns the lead score and improves it from your won and lost deals.
- **Outreach:** first messages, follow-ups and replies, in your voice, about the buyer's situation, never about you.
- **Deal support:** call prep, discovery questions, proposals and the follow-up after.
- **Pipeline:** honest counts, response times and a forecast that says which numbers are real and which are still guesses.
- **Reviewer:** checks everything buyer-facing before you see it, and makes the strongest case against it.

## What makes it different
1. **Your method, not ours.** The team thinks with four short files: how you read buyers, the strategy choices every business answers, how you rate evidence, and your own rules for selling. Each starts with a starter. Replace them with yours, and the whole team works your way.
2. **A lead score that learns from your own wins.** Every lead gets a fit score and a timing score. Once 20 deals have closed, won or lost, the fit scorer checks which scores turned into wins, lists every miss, and improves the model one proven change at a time, on your yes.
3. **Strategy, not just activity.** Growth reports start with what's changing in your market, name the three problems in the way, and give a strategy for each, with a cheap test before you commit.
4. **Inbound and outbound.** It finds buyers from public signals, drafts replies to the leads that come to you, or both, and reports each separately.
5. **Exact math, no code to trust.** Written methods tell Claude exactly how to score, dedupe and count, and Claude calculates with its own code tool, so the numbers come out the same every time. Scaevola Sales ships no scripts: nothing from this repository runs on your computer.
6. **Your rules hold.** Text inside an email, a web page or a CRM note is information, never an instruction. Opt-outs are checked before every list. Each business's files stay apart.

## A weekly rhythm
Every Monday, the team can review the week for you on a schedule: what was sent, replies, meetings, deals, and the one decision waiting on you. It's its own scheduled task, and it never sends anything. Bolted on to Scaevola, it's written for your CRO seat.

## What you need
- **A paid Claude plan (Pro or above),** for Claude Code. The assistants and the weekly review use your plan's usage like any chat; heavy prospecting may need a higher plan.
- **A free GitHub account.** No account yet? The prompt walks you through it.
- **Optional: the tools you already sell with,** such as your CRM, your email or a contact data tool, connected to Claude if you want the team to read from them. It works with any CRM you can connect to Claude or export to a spreadsheet. It drafts there only where you allow it, and never sends.
- **Before you send at volume:** send from a separate, warmed-up domain, so your main domain's reputation stays safe. The [compliance playbook](sales/playbooks/compliance.md) has the inbox rules and a pre-send checklist.

## How to start
1. Open [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md), the installer, and click **Copy raw file** (the copy icon above the file).
2. Open a new chat at claude.ai and paste it.
3. Answer one question at a time. It tells you each next step.

Named after Scaevola, the fan flower whose blooms look like half a flower. You bring the half that knows your buyers. The sales team does the finding, drafting and counting.

It's not a hire, and it never sends anything on its own.

**Honest limits:** it works only with Claude and GitHub. It finds and drafts; sending is yours, from your own tools. The lead score improves only from your own closed deals.
