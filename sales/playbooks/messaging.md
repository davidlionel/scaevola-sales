# Messaging

How the outreach agent writes, and what the reviewer checks. The business's `voice.md` sets the tone; the motion repo can change the angle. These rules hold for every business.

## The first message
1. **Their trigger first.** Open with something the buyer can check about their own situation: "Saw you're hiring a second ops lead in Denver." Never open with us.
2. **The problem, framed as an outside enemy.** Name what that moment usually causes, as something that happens to everyone in their spot, never as their failure.
3. **One line of proof,** from `offer.md` only. It should make them feel safer, not make us look impressive.
4. **One small ask,** easy to say yes to: a question, a short call, or permission to send something.
5. **Short.** A few sentences a person can read on a phone. (Practice, not a rule backed by evidence; the business's results decide.)

## Follow-ups
- At most two, each adding something new (a useful resource, a different angle on the same trigger, or a simple "should I close this?").
- Space them out; the play plan sets the days.
- Never "just bumping this", never guilt.

## The lens in the message
- **Fit the culture code:** use the buyer's own words from `icp.md`, not ours.
- **Loss framing, honestly:** what they risk losing if the moment passes, only where the evidence supports it.
- **First impression:** the opening should make them feel understood, not exposed.
- **Anxiety and habit:** make the ask small enough that saying yes feels safe.
- **No false urgency,** fake scarcity or invented deadlines. Ever.

## Subject lines
Plain and accurate (required by law in the US; see [compliance.md](compliance.md)). Often their trigger in a few words. No tricks such as "Re:" on a first message.

## Replies
- **Positive:** answer fast, propose a time, and hand off to [deal support](../.claude/agents/deal-support.md) for prep.
- **"Not now":** thank them, ask when to check back, and log it in the tally.
- **"Already covered":** thank them and log the reason; it's evidence about the offer.
- **Opt-out:** stop at once, and add them to the suppression list.

## Inbound
A lead who came to us gets a different first message: answer what they asked, then one next step, no unasked pitch. See [inbound.md](inbound.md).

## Writing rules
Plain words, short sentences, no em dashes, no AI tells (no "I hope this finds you well", no "delve", no lists of three adjectives). The buyer is the hero. For the owner's own businesses, also follow the owner's writing style, linked in the business's `voice.md`.
