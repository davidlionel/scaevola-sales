# Public signals

Where the prospector finds people who have the problem now. A signal is something that just happened and is publicly checkable. It shows a struggling moment (push), not just a fit (size, industry).

## Rules
- **Every row records** the signal, its date and the source link. No link, no row.
- **Freshness:** each play sets a window (for example, the last 30 days). Older signals are dropped, because urgency fades.
- **One trigger per play.** Mixing triggers makes the results unreadable.
- **Public and allowed only.** No leaked or personal (non-work) data; work contact details only from providers the business approved, and only after the signal check. No scraping where a site's terms forbid it. See [compliance.md](compliance.md).
- **Inbound leads bring their own signal:** the action they took. They follow [inbound.md](inbound.md), not a list.
- **Signals suggest; they don't prove.** The pain a signal points to is a hypothesis until a buyer confirms it. Mark it that way in the call prep.

## Common signals

| Signal | What it can mean (hypothesis) | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| A job post for a role | They're short a person and the work is piling up; or they're building something new | Company careers pages, job boards |
| A key person left or joined | A new leader wants quick wins; a departure leaves gaps | Company announcements, press releases, public profiles |
| New business formed or registered | Everything needs setting up at once | State business registries, regulator filings (for example SEC adviser filings) |
| Funding raised | Pressure to grow fast and show results | Press releases, funding databases |
| Expansion: new office, location, product or market | New work the current setup wasn't built for | Press releases, local news, permits |
| Merger or acquisition | Systems and teams to combine | Press releases, filings |
| A regulation or deadline that affects them | A date they can't move | Regulator sites, trade press |
| A tool change or end-of-life date | A forced switch with a deadline | Vendor announcements, the company's own posts |
| Public complaints or reviews about a problem we solve | Pain their customers can see | Review sites, public posts |
| The buyer posting about the problem | They're already looking for help | Public posts, forums, community threads |

Each business's `icp.md` names the one or two signals that fit its trigger. A motion repo can add its own (for example, product usage for PLG).

## Checking a signal
1. Open the source. Does it say what the row says?
2. Is the date inside the play's window?
3. Does it match the trigger in `icp.md`, or just the buyer type?
4. Rate it: strong (primary source, such as a filing or the company's own post), moderate (trade press), weak (aggregator or second-hand).
