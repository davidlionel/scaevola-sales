# Evidence rules

How the team rates what it knows. A starter: replace or tighten it whenever you like.

## Rate every claim
- **Strong:** a primary source (a filing, the company's own words, your own records), or a pattern across many independent sources.
- **Moderate:** a reputable secondary source, or a pattern across a few.
- **Weak:** one anecdote, an aggregator, or a source with a reason to say it.
- **Hypothesis:** our best guess, marked as one until it's tested.

## Rules
- Every number traces to a source or a file. A gap is written "unknown", never filled.
- **Twenty records before a rate.** With fewer, report the count and say "small sample". [calculations.md](../team/calculations.md) applies this to every rate.
- Say who benefits from a claim being believed, and weigh it.
- Look for the evidence against a plan, not only the evidence for it.
- What buyers did (booked, showed, paid) outranks what they said.
