# House rules

Your own rules for selling. They apply to every business you run. A client's business can record a difference in its `business.md`, with the client's OK.

## Rules every team keeps
- Agents draft; a person sends. Nothing goes out without a person.
- Never invent experience, results, proof, client names, numbers or dates.
- Problem before tool: sell the outcome, not the software or the process.
- Acute pain first: start where the buyer already feels the problem.
