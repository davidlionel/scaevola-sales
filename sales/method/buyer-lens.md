# Buyer lens

The questions the team asks about every buyer. A starter: replace it with your own lens whenever you like. Whatever is here, every finding must end in a choice: **"because [finding], we will [message, offer, price anchor, channel, first line, proof, or what we drop]."** A finding that changes nothing is cut. Mark each line evidence or hypothesis.

## Starter questions
1. **The moment:** what just happened that makes this a problem now? What do they do when it happens?
2. **The job:** what are they trying to get done: the practical job, how they want to feel, and how they want to be seen?
3. **Said vs meant:** what do they say they want, and what does it mean to them underneath?
4. **The fear:** what are they afraid of, or afraid of being blamed for, if they act or if they don't?
5. **Who they listen to:** whose example or approval makes acting feel normal and safe?
6. **First impression:** what does our offer feel like in the first five seconds? What would they lose by waiting?
7. **What holds them back:** what worries them about switching, and what habit keeps them where they are?

## Rules
- Honest only: no false urgency, no invented scarcity, no fear the evidence doesn't support.
- Ask buyers for stories, not opinions ("tell me about the last time..."), and keep their words.
- The outside enemy is the problem, never the buyer.
