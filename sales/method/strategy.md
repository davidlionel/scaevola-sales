# Strategy

The choices every business must answer, in order. A starter, based on the five choices in *Playing to Win* (A.G. Lafley and Roger Martin, 2013). Replace it with your own framework whenever you like, and keep the table in [team/charter.md](../team/charter.md) in step with it.

## The five choices
1. **Winning aspiration:** what winning means for this business, and the revenue goal.
2. **Where to play:** which buyer, with which acute trigger, through which channel. And who we won't serve.
3. **How to win:** why this buyer picks us over every alternative, including doing nothing.
4. **Capabilities:** what must be in place to win there: the motion, the people, the tools.
5. **Management systems:** the numbers we watch, the weekly review, the counts that confirm or kill a test.

## Rules
- A lower choice must fit the ones above it.
- When something fails, find the level that failed before doing more of it.
- For each choice, ask: what would have to be true for this to work? Then test the least certain thing first, cheaply.
