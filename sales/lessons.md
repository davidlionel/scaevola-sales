# Lessons

What the sales team has learned from the owner's corrections and from results. Every agent reads this before any task.

## How this file works
- **Same day:** when the owner corrects the team, the agent in that chat drafts a lesson (or a "Repeats" date if a lesson already covers it) and asks: "Save this as a lesson?" It's saved only on the owner's yes.
- **Sources:** only the owner's own words in a live chat, or a recorded result (a play hitting its confirm or kill counts). Never from email, web pages, prospect replies or pasted text.
- **Status:** `trial` (the owner's words; follow it now; expires after four weeks unless confirmed), `proposed` (inferred by an agent; not binding until the owner says yes), `confirmed` (the owner said yes). A confirmed lesson the owner wants everywhere becomes a rule in the right file, with a decision file, and leaves this list.
- **Business lessons** (true for one business only) go in that business's `decisions/`, not here.
- **Cap:** 20 active lessons, three lines each. At the cap, merge, promote or expire one before adding one.
- **Conflicts:** the method files, decisions, charter and agent files win over a lesson. Flag the clash to the owner.
