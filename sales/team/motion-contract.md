# Motion contract

What a motion repo (PLG, MLG, SLG, customer-led) adds to the core team, and the rules it follows. The core team knows sales; a motion repo makes it better at one way of selling.

## How they work together
Repos don't talk to each other. A working session opens the sales repository plus the motion add-on(s) named in the business's `motion.md`. The sales manager runs the business and calls on the motion's specialists at set points:

1. **The motion step (every business, plays or reports):** the motion's `assessment.md` scores the business's current approach against the motion's best practice, and the motion's playbook shapes the business's `motion.md`, targets and stages.
2. **Play plan:** the motion says where buyers come from (sign-ups, content, named accounts, customers) and what counts as a good signal.
3. **Drafts:** core agents write in the motion's style. The motion's reviewer checks fit before the core reviewer.
4. **Weekly review:** the motion adds its own numbers to the scorecard.
5. **Growth reports:** the scorecard from the motion step feeds the report's problems and strategies, and the motion's reviewer checks them.

## What a motion repo must have
- `CLAUDE.md` that points to the method/ folder and to this contract.
- `playbook.md`: how this motion wins, when it fits and when it doesn't, and what it changes for each core role.
- `.claude/agents/`: specialists only this motion needs (for PLG, for example: usage signals and the free-to-paid path).
- `metrics.md`: the motion's numbers, with definitions, added to the core scorecard.
- `motion-template.md`: the section the strategist fills in a business's `motion.md`.
- `assessment.md`: the motion's best-practice scorecard: what good looks like, item by item; how to score a business against each item (with the evidence used); the motion's numbers to pull; and the usual fixes. Run at the motion step for every business, including businesses that only want reports ([method](../playbooks/growth-report.md)).
- Templates and examples, each marked with its source and evidence strength.
- A `VERSION` file. The business's `motion.md` records which version it uses.

## What a motion repo must not do
- Rebuild a core role (prospecting, outreach, deal support, pipeline, review). It extends them.
- Hold client data. Know-how only, so it can be shared or sold safely.
- Copy the owner's method files. Link to them.
- Override the charter's "never" list. Agents draft; people send.

## Blends
A business can open two motion repos (for example, PLG for sign-ups and SLG for larger accounts). `motion.md` names which motion owns which buyers and stages, so no buyer gets two sequences.
