# Businesses

Each business the team sells for is a business: the owner's own businesses and clients alike. Each gets one folder, `businesses/<name>/`, holding its pack, its plays and its decisions.

## Start a business
1. Copy [_template/](_template/README.md) to `businesses/<name>/` (lowercase, hyphens).
2. Ask the [strategist](../.claude/agents/strategist.md) to run the intake and fill the pack.
3. The decider approves `icp.md`, `offer.md`, `motion.md` and `targets.md`. Only then does the [sales manager](../.claude/agents/sales-manager.md) plan the first play.
4. If the business runs inbound, follow [playbooks/inbound-go-live.md](../playbooks/inbound-go-live.md) before the first lead arrives, including the scheduled task.
5. Add a line for the business below and in [index.md](../index.md).

## Rules
- **Client material stays in its folder.** Never carry contacts, proof, prices or plans from one business into another.
- **Linked, not copied.** If a business's strategy already lives elsewhere (for example in a Scaevola team repository), `business.md` links to those files and the team reads them each time.
- **Confidential clients:** if a client's terms require it, their pack moves to its own private repo and this list keeps only its name and a link.
