# [Business name]

## Who
- **Business:** what it sells, in one line.
- **Type:** the owner's own, or client.
- **Decider:** who approves the pack, plays and every send.
- **Sender(s):** who sends messages and runs calls.
- **Engagement:** plays, growth reports, or both.

## Winning (cascade choice 1)
- **Revenue goal:** amount and by when. Source.
- **What winning means here:** for example, a repeatable channel, a first 10 customers, or a predictable monthly number.

## Linked sources (read each time; never copy)
- For example: the business's own repo, strategy files, site, past messages.

## Executive team (if bolted on to Scaevola)
Leave out if this team runs on its own. See [team/operating-modes.md](../../team/operating-modes.md). When bolted on, the sales manager reports to the CRO seat in Scaevola's `seats.md`.

- **Strategy files this pack links to:** `customer-profile.md`, `pricing-rules.md`, `plan.md`, `seats.md` and `decisions/` in that repository.
- **Extra rules from that repository:** anything in its house rules or lessons that changes how this team sells.
